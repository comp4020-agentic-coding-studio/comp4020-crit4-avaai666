# Listening pass

Run this before accepting any change that touches audio. Same order every
time. Record the date and the result.

1. Silence on load. Open the page. Nothing sounds until I click.
2. First strike. The sound starts on press, not on release. No perceptible
   gap.
3. Two tones. On one bell, centre and corner give two clearly different
   pitches, and the gap sounds like a third.
4. In between. Striking between centre and corner gives both pitches at
   once, not a crossfade artefact.
5. Sweep. Dragging across the rack produces no clicks, no crackle, no
   dropouts.
6. Pile-up. Strike ten bells as fast as I can. No clipping, no distortion,
   no mush.
7. Tail. Let one bell ring out untouched. It decays smoothly to silence
   with no cut-off click.
8. Bronze or beep. Does it read as struck metal, or as an oscillator?
9. Keyboard. asdfghjkl; and qwertyuiop all sound, and the q row is audibly
   above the a row.
10. 390x844. All nine checks again at phone size, on the deployed URL.
